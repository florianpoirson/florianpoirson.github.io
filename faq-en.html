Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting in touch.

Are sessions held in person?
No. All training is delivered online by video call, in any country and time zone.
Which languages are taught?
British and American English, and French as a foreign language. Sessions can be conducted in French or English depending on your goal.
How much does a programme cost?
Fees are set individually after a free diagnostic call, depending on format, frequency and total volume. A written quote is provided before the first session.
What level do I need to start?
From rebuilding the basics to native-level refinement. It suits people who already have a good level and are stuck on cultural usage and naturalness.
Which examinations can be prepared?
The CAPES and agrégation in English, grandes écoles papers and orals, admissions interviews, and professional orals in English.
What is the executive-level programme?
Four modules for executives, experts and diplomats: executive speaking and leadership, negotiation and diplomacy, strategic writing, and accent reduction and delivery.
What does the intercultural approach change?
We work on what your audience expects: how direct you may be, the role of humour, how to express disagreement, what is said and what goes unsaid. That is the difference between being understood and being convincing.
